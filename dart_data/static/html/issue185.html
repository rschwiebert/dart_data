Subject / Target Issue 185
Target Citation Matsumura, Hideyuki (2022). Commutative algebra. 2nd Ed. (14.E) & (34.B), p. 258.
Claim: "The ring $A$ of (14.E) is a G-ring which is not u.c."
Statement Established The claim does not hold when $k$ is perfect with positive characteristic ($\operatorname{char}(k) > 0$). Under these parameters, $A$ is not a G-ring.

Theorem:

Proof

Setting (14.E). $S=k[[x]]$; $z=\sum_{i\ge1}a_ix^i\in S$ transcendental over $k(x)$; $z_j=(z-\sum_{i\le j}a_ix^i)/x^{j-1}$; $R=k[x,z_1,z_2,\dots]\subset S$; $\mathfrak m=(x)$, $\mathfrak n=(x-1,z_1,z_2,\dots)$; $B$ the localization of $R$ at $(R\setminus\mathfrak m)\cap(R\setminus\mathfrak n)$; $A=k+\text{rad} B$. Matsumura proves: $B$ is a finite $A$-module, $D:=B_{\mathfrak mB}=R_{\mathfrak m}$ is a regular local ring of dimension 1 with maximal ideal $xD$, $R/\mathfrak m=k$, and $R\subset k(x,z)$. Put $K=k(x,z)$, $K'=k((x))$. $k$ is perfect of $\operatorname{char}(k) > 0$.

Step 1: Since $x,z\in R\subset K$, $\operatorname{Frac} D=K$; $D$ is a DVR with uniformizer $x$ and residue field $k$, so $K=D[1/x]$. For each $n$, $D/x^nD\to S/x^nS$ is surjective (as $k[x]\subset D$) between $k$-spaces of dimension $n$, hence bijective; so $D\subset S$ induces $\widehat D\cong S$, and $\widehat D\otimes_DK=S[1/x]=K'$.

Step 2: Let $L=K(\xi,\zeta)$ with $\xi^p=x$, $\zeta^p=z$. We show $K'\otimes_KL$ is not reduced. Since $\xi,\zeta$ are algebraically independent over $k$, $[L:K]=[k(\xi,\zeta):k(\xi^p,\zeta^p)]=p^2$, so $K'\otimes_KL=K'[\xi,\zeta]/(\xi^p-x,\zeta^p-z)$ is free over $K'$ with basis $\xi^a\zeta^b$, $0\le a,b\le p$. Sorting $z$ by exponents mod $p$ and using $a_i\in k^p$,

$$ z=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}x^j e_j^{\,p},\qquad e_j=\sum_{i\equiv j\ (p)}a_i^{1/p}\,x^{(i-j)/p}\in S\subset K'. $$

Put $w=\sum_j e_j\xi^j$. Then $w^p=\sum_j e_j^{\,p}x^j=z=\zeta^p$, so $(\zeta-w)^p=0$, while $\zeta-w\neq0$ (its coefficient on the basis element $\zeta$ is $1$). (Equivalently: MacLane's criterion, Ex. 27.1 / Stacks 030W(3), fails, so $K'/K$ is inseparable.)

Step 3: Conclusion. If $A$ were a G-ring, so would be the finite $A$-algebra $B$ (Th. 77, (33.G)) and its localization $D$ ((33.A)); then $D\to\widehat D$ is regular, so by (33.A) its fibre over $(0)$, which is $K'$ by Step 1, satisfies: $K'\otimes_KL$ is regular, in particular reduced, for every finite $L/K$, contradicting Step 2. $\square$
